## Disclosed exceptions and caveats

1. **Item 7 — read/hold SNM post-layout (PEX) gap.** No PEX-compatible path
   exists for read SNM or hold SNM (Seevinck's method needs to break the
   cross-coupled loop; no drawn boundary isolates one storage inverter).
   Write access time, read access time and write margin are closed
   post-layout, 27/27 corners. Closing the gap needs a new isolated-inverter
   layout. Full investigation: [`../sim/pex/README.md`](../sim/pex/README.md).
   This is a missing measurement, not a measured miss; the operator accepted
   it as a bronze-compatible exception.
2. **Machine grader reads 10/11, not 11/11, and that is a tooling gap, not a
   substance gap.** The pinned `klayout-tools` 0.5.0 predates item-11 grading
   rules, so the item-11 envelope renders `unrecognized_envelope`; and the
   release's own item-11 requirement of a checked `ties[]` supply spec is
   pending (see `signoff/README.md`, item 11 row).
3. **Items 1, 2, 9, 10 are cited for the grader, not proven by it.** These
   items have no `klt` verb. `klt signoff` 0.5.0 accepts any passing native
   envelope and rejects `kind: generic` for them, so they are cited (issue
   #126) with adjacent native envelopes; the substance remains the human audit
   in the 2026-08-22 re-read. See `signoff/README.md` for each row.
